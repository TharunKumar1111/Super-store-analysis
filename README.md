# 🛒 Super Store Sales Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analytics project using **Python, MySQL, and Power BI** to analyze retail sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and business trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a Super Store retail dataset containing 9,994 records and 21 columns. The objective is to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate product performance, and uncover business opportunities through data analysis and visualization.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow, starting with data cleaning and progressing to SQL-based business analysis and Power BI dashboard development.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, orders, and quantity.
- Identify the highest revenue-generating customer segments.
- Evaluate sales performance across shipping modes.
- Analyze category and sub-category performance.
- Identify top-performing and loss-making products.
- Compare sales performance across states and regions.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and repeat customers.

## 📂 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning – Python

- Imported the raw Excel dataset using Pandas.
- Inspected dataset structure and data types.
- Checked missing values and duplicate records.
- Standardized column names.
- Validated numerical and date columns.
- Exported the cleaned dataset into CSV format.

### 2. SQL Business Analysis – MySQL

Performed business analysis using SQL queries covering:

- Overall business performance.
- Category and product analysis.
- Customer segment analysis.
- Shipping performance.
- Geographical sales analysis.
- Year-wise and monthly sales trends.
- Discount and profitability analysis.
- Customer behavior and repeat purchases.

### 3. Dashboard Development – Power BI

Developed a dashboard to visualize key business metrics and sales performance.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity

**Dashboard Visualizations**

- Segment-wise Sales
- Ship Mode-wise Sales
- Sub-Category-wise Sales
- State-wise Sales

## 📊 Key Business Findings

The analysis focuses on identifying:

- Customer segments contributing the most revenue.
- Product categories and sub-categories driving sales.
- Shipping methods associated with higher sales.
- States generating the highest revenue.
- Products and sub-categories with negative profitability.
- Discount levels associated with lower profit margins.
- Sales trends across different years and months.
